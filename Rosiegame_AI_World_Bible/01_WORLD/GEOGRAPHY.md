# Geography — BROAD INSPIRATION ESTABLISHED; MAP PROVISIONAL

Use Europe as loose inspiration, not a one-to-one copy.

- **Old Kingdom:** France-like, farther south/southwest; older civilised heartland.
- **New Empire:** southeast-England/London-like; younger and expansionist.
- **Old Peoples:** scattered across borderlands, uplands, forests, islands, marshes and mountains.
- **Everlasting Court:** Scandinavia is a possible inspiration. Remote islands, fjords and mountains protect isolated strongholds. This is not an openly ruling evil Scandinavian kingdom.
- **Black Rampart:** a vast natural barrier of mountains, glaciers, cliffs and ravines, provisionally northeast of the Court's principal territory.
- **Dead Marches:** dangerous territory beyond the Black Rampart, potentially connected with the Undying Host.

Only a few passes may permit large armies through the Black Rampart. The Court's strongholds may be almost impossible to conquer directly, but it can raid, infiltrate, manipulate politics and target resources. Its influence is disproportionate, not unlimited.
