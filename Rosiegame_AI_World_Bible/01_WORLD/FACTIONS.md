# Factions and Networks — WORKING MODEL

The current model has four broad populations/powers and two networks. They are not all the same kind of organisation.

1. **Hidden Society of Traditional Magic** — PROVISIONAL name/structure. A secret society, especially within the Old Kingdom, preserves older magic and trains practitioners. It is not automatically virtuous.
2. **Everlasting Court Infiltration Network** — ESTABLISHED concept. Spies, informants, compromised officials, merchants, intermediaries and coerced agents extend Court influence. Some know the organisation; others are manipulated or used. This is a network, not a separate people.
3. **Old Peoples** — ESTABLISHED broad concept. Distinct clans, tribal kingdoms and rural communities across borderlands, uplands, forests, islands, marshes and other margins. They are not unified; some resist, trade, cooperate or assimilate.
4. **Old Kingdom** — ESTABLISHED broad concept. An ancient civilisation with old roads, cities, law, engineering, professional armies and institutions. It coexisted with magic for centuries. It has practical experience but also rigidity, class divisions, decay and ambition.
5. **New Empire** — ESTABLISHED broad concept. A younger, centralised, expansionist power with a southeast-England/London-like inspiration. It values administration, law, order, productivity and material progress; it is powerful but can be rash and overconfident.
6. **Everlasting Court** — ESTABLISHED concept; details provisional. Defeated magic users associated with the old royal faction, surviving in remote northern strongholds and through agents. Not a conventional kingdom. Cooperation is maintained by self-interest, shared secrets, debts, reciprocal favours and fear of betrayal. It seeks some combination of pleasure, power, longevity/immortality, resources and forbidden knowledge. It is not omnipotent.

**Important distinction:** the Everlasting Court is not the same as the Undying Host. Not every person affected by dark magic belongs to the Court, and not every Hollowed person is necessarily part of the Host.

**Open:** the Old Kingdom's exact role in the war; the Court's relationship with the Undying Host; the Hidden Society's relationship with the Old Kingdom.
