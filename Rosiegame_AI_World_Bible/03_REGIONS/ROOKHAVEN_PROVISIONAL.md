# Rookhaven — First Playable Region — PROVISIONAL

The plan is to develop one compact region fully first, treating it as if it were the whole game. It should support exploration, conversations, practical missions, mysteries and consequences.

Possible layout:
- **Port town:** market square, tavern, blacksmith, traders, fishermen, small garrison and imperial customs house.
- **Hinterland:** farms, hamlets, woodland and a landholder's estate.
- **Coast:** coves, sea caves, abandoned watchtower; possible smuggling and raiding.
- **Older sites:** overgrown shrine, ancient earthworks and pre-Empire ruins.

The town belongs to the New Empire. Magic is prohibited, but residents' views differ and rural customs survive.

Possible starting tensions (examples only): cargoes passing through customs unchecked; an innkeeper who owes favours; a soldier ordering unaccounted-for equipment; a woodland-rights dispute; a magistrate investigating smuggling but avoiding scandal; a healer using inherited remedies; a retired soldier who knows an official account is false; a merchant with a northern contact.

Design goal: make the region feel like a functioning place even when the player is absent. NPCs need needs, schedules, relationships, local knowledge and independent goals.
