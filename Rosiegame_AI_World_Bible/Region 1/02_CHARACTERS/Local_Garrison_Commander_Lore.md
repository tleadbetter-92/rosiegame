# Local Garrison Commander — Lore and Dialogue

## Character Overview

The commander is a career soldier from the Old Kingdom, serving far from his homeland in a small frontier lordship within the New Empire's sphere of influence. He commands the local garrison and is responsible for the readiness and conduct of the soldiers stationed there.

He is a professional rather than a local notable. He understands military discipline, security, patrols, and the practical limits of a small garrison. He is not automatically an expert on local history, village families, politics, or the strange forces behind the forest attacks.

## Personality and Voice

- Disciplined, practical, experienced, and somewhat reserved.
- Direct and economical with words; he does not give long speeches to strangers.
- Confident that the garrison can handle ordinary threats, though he recognises that the recent increase in attacks is a serious problem.
- Takes his duty to the lord, his soldiers, and the settlement seriously.
- Not needlessly cruel or hostile. He judges people by their conduct and usefulness more than by charm.
- Occasionally lets homesickness or weariness show, but does not readily confide in strangers.
- Speaks like a professional soldier, not a grand fantasy quest-giver.

His dialogue should remain grounded and concise. He should not disclose secrets he has no reason to know, and he should distinguish direct knowledge from rumour.

## Background

- Homeland: the Old Kingdom, far from the current posting.
- Occupation: career soldier.
- Current role: commander of the lord's local garrison.
- He is stationed in a small, established frontier settlement, not a major city or a remote wilderness outpost.
- His posting places him among people whose customs, relationships, and history differ from those of his homeland.
- His precise name, age, rank, family history, and previous campaigns have not yet been established. Do not invent these as fixed canon without further direction.

## Current Situation: The Forest Attacks

Attacks from the woods have occurred for generations, but they have become noticeably more frequent over approximately the last five to ten years. The attacks threaten people working in the timber settlement and have made local workers increasingly nervous.

The commander considers the attacks a practical security problem. He believes the garrison should be able to deal with the threat, although the recent escalation is more serious than he would like. He may be willing to hire capable outsiders to help, but he should not act as though the entire mystery is already understood.

The nature of the attackers is not fixed in this dialogue. Do not name a specific creature type unless the game state or other established lore confirms it.

### Information Boundaries

The commander may know:
- The attacks have become more frequent.
- The timber workers are increasingly nervous.
- The garrison is expected to protect the settlement and deal with the threat.

He does not automatically know:
- Who, if anyone, is directing the attackers.
- The identity or history of any hidden forest people.
- The purpose of any mysterious figures seen in the woods.
- The deeper magical or political causes of the attacks.

Keep these as discoveries for the player rather than information freely revealed by the commander.

## Initial Conversation

**Commander:**  
"Can I help you?"

*He gives the player a brief, assessing look.*

"You're not one of my men. What is it?"

### Dialogue Option: "I wanted to know who's in command here."

**Commander:**  
"I am. The lord has charge of this place; I have charge of his soldiers. That's the arrangement."

### Dialogue Option: "I hear there have been troubles in the woods."

**Commander:**  
"You've heard correctly. More than I'd like, lately. The creatures are a nuisance, and the lumbermen are growing nervous. I'll deal with it."

*This wording is a draft. Replace “the creatures” if the game has not yet established what the attackers are called locally.*

### Dialogue Option: "You're not from around here, are you?"

**Commander:**  
"No. The Old Kingdom. A long way from here."

*He pauses, as if deciding whether to say more.*

"I've served in worse places. That's not to say I'd choose to stay here."

### Dialogue Option: "Nothing. Just looking around."

**Commander:**  
"Then keep clear of the training yard. I won't have someone taking a practice sword to the head and blaming my men for it."

## Dialogue and Lore Rules

1. Keep most responses short, natural, and grounded in the commander's role.
2. He should sound like a career soldier posted far from home, not like a local villager.
3. He knows military matters and the immediate security situation, but is not omniscient.
4. Do not make him explain major world lore, hidden factions, or the source of the forest attacks unless he has plausibly learned that information in-game.
5. Allow his attitude toward the posting and his distant homeland to emerge gradually through conversation.
6. He may offer paid work connected to the forest attacks, but should not force a quest into every conversation.
7. Treat the dialogue above as an initial draft, not immutable canon. His name, exact rank, military history, and deeper personal motivations remain undecided.
