# Rookhaven Tales — EXAMPLE ONLY

These stories were drafted as inspiration, not confirmed canon. They can be rewritten, split apart or discarded. Do not assume they all happened or connect to one conspiracy.

1. **The Man Who Came Back:** Edric Vale returns after twenty-three years looking unchanged. He warns his son that some people offer wealth, healing, youth and long life, but eventually one stops caring about the price. He vanishes; his son finds a black mark on his wrist: a circle broken by three lines. Possible Court connection, unresolved.
2. **The Witch at the Spring:** Mara, a rural healer, saves a magistrate's daughter with an inherited remedy that may involve magic. The magistrate later dismisses an uncertain case against her and never explains why.
3. **The Customs House:** Customs master Othmar Pell lets suspicious cargo through after a captain threatens his son. Cargo includes black sand, old coins and untitled books. Othmar reports the captain, but the ship and his son disappear. Possible Court-network connection.
4. **The Bell Beneath the Ruins:** Three boys ring a silent bronze bell; a deep bell answers underground. Years later, a stranger asks whether it rang three times. It rang twice. He says, “Then there may still be time,” and leaves another bell.
5. **A Job for the Garrison:** Captain Serah Venn investigates a warehouse theft, suspects a northern clan mark was planted, and finds a symbol like a circle broken by three lines. Possible Court connection.
6. **The Man Who Sold His Shadow:** A merchant trades his shadow for youth and health, then watches his family age while he remains young. A fisherman later sees the merchant's shadow standing several paces away.

Rumours: lights beneath the water near the watchtower; imperial timber-taking angering a farmer; a merchant paying twice the worth for old books; a body wearing a two-hundred-year-old uniform; a voice in the woods sounding like a relative; a scholar asking who saved people before the Empire; an innkeeper refusing rooms to visitors who pay in old coins and leave before dawn.

Keep these threads independent unless the player's actions create a credible connection.
