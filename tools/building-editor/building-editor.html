Building editor

Town editor

2 m grid — Shift + click to place or erase. Holding Shift keeps the view still. WASD pans. R rotate. Q/E height ±0.5 m (half square, for tables); Shift+Q/E jump a full storey.

Floors & props (megakit)
  • Floor — Floor_Mosaic_PolyHaven = real CC0 photo mosaic (Poly Haven). Procedural: Floor_Mosaic_Terra, BlackWhite, Medallion, Meander. Same 2 m tile; R to rotate.
  • OverhangCorner / OverhangCorner2 — same L-floor; 2 is the opposite corner (mirrored lip). Use 1 on one outer corner of a deck, 2 on the diagonally opposite outer corner; use R on each cell so lips face open air.
  • Prop — crates, wagon, chimneys, vines, fences, border trim; search prop or floor above.
  • Furnish (1 m) mode — big furniture on 1 m grid; small items (mugs, books, candles) on 0.5 m. Arrow keys nudge the last placed furnish piece (0.25 m; Shift 0.5; Ctrl 0.1).
  • Lantern_Wall / Torch_Metal — wall edge + 1 m along the wall; R to align with the wall run.
  • Medieval street lamps are not in the village kit — use the town editor lamp or CC0 under models/town-props/.
Walls & corners (megakit)
  1. Wall types in this kit — only three straight-wall looks: Wall_Plaster (smooth), Wall_Brick (UnevenBrick), and Wall_Plaster_WoodGrid (half-timber). There are no separate stone or red-brick straight walls in the megakit.
  2. Red-brick look — mix Floor_RedBrick, Corner_Exterior_Brick, DoorFrame_*_Brick, Roof_Front_Brick* with plaster or uneven-brick walls.
  3. Lay Floor_* on tile centres. Walls, wall windows, Window_*, shutters, DoorFrame_* use edge snap like straights; R for outward face.
  4. Corners: Corner_Exterior_Brick / Wood, or Straight_L / _R caps.
How to roof (megakit)
  1. Q/E to the top floor (same footprint as walls).
  2. Roof → Roof RoundTiles N×M — same cell-centre snap as floors; size name ≈ metres (4×4 ≈ 4 m).
  3. Overhang straight and side caps — same edge as the wall on that square. R points the lip outward. Short sticks out about 1 m, Long about 2 m. Place L or R on the same spot as the straight; they are the two ends of that square.
  4. Overhang corner — sits on the corner of the square, where two walls meet. Corner Front goes on the same corner. R turns it.
  5. Overhang roof and roof incline — still the middle of the square.
  6. Roof → Roof Front Brick4/6/8 — gable end; rotate to face the end wall.

Floor level: 1 (y = 0)

by storey

Squares are 2 m. Storey 1 is the ground. Storey 2 is the next floor, so it can sit on a ground room. Aim the cursor where the corner should go. No doors, windows, or roof. Undo removes the whole room.
