Campaign Village

What the strategy side sees

Board

You are the Southwest campaign player. Train a number of units, then choose how many to send and click a wood stand or an ore. Units are not drawn on the map. End your turn when you are finished.

Place a building

    Yellow spot: a house or a farm. Lumberyard: click a wood tick. Mine: click an ore. Those two can sit outside the village. Each 100 m from Southwest takes 1% off 120 a day, and that loss is rounded up. Square with numbers: a building you ordered. White circle: you. Pale square: the town walls. These orders stay on this page. They are not sent to the war.
