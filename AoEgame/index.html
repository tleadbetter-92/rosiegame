Age of Empires

Placing the two manors…

WASD slides the map. Drag with the left button to select. Drag with the right button and the ground follows your hand. A right-click sends them. A building takes 5 minutes. A worker or soldier takes 1 minute. At 9 pm workers go into a house, up to 20 in each, and come back out at 5 am. One real minute is one hour. Press F to walk as yourself: the crosshair is where you look, Space jumps, left-click swings or shoots, P opens the pack (sword, axe, halberd, mace, bow and crossbow). Hitting a side makes them your enemy until you fall. A small house costs 20 wood and holds 5 more workers. A barracks holds 15 soldiers. Houses stay inside the ring around the manor. A barracks can sit anywhere, and selecting it trains soldiers. Right-click a farm and up to 5 people work the field, then drop the food at its house. Right-click ore for gold. A salt works can sit by the trees or water, and people drop off there when it is closer. Barracks, farm, salt works and the storehouse cost 100 wood. Soldiers: Guard or Attack.
