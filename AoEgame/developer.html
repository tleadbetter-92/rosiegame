Units

Unit In Game use Graphic model used Stats Animations Cost Base size In use Look

These are the soldier and the worker already in the match. Health, attack, move, and cost are kept in this browser. Reload the match after a change. Health and cost apply to the next unit trained. Move and attack apply to units already on the map. Attack is the damage a soldier deals. Defence is taken off that hit: attack 8 against defence 2 deals 6. It applies to units already on the map, and a worker’s defence counts when they are hit. Workers do not fight yet, so a worker’s attack is kept for later. Base size is the footprint on the map, width by depth in metres. It starts as the size of that person. Change it and reload the match, and the person changes with it. Tick In use yourself once you have seen that one placed and are happy with it. In Game use is military, economical, or NPC. Food is the cost the match charges. + resource adds another, such as gold, and the match charges that too. Training time is seconds. Set that column to Number, type the time, then Commit changes. The next unit trained uses it. A column named Created at lists the buildings, and a unit can tick more than one, such as Barracks and Manor. A building trains the units listed on its row. The manor starts with the worker, and the barracks with the soldier. The match can field the soldier and the worker. Graphic model lists the animated characters and animals. Pick one for a unit, such as Blacksmith, and See shows that model. The match still uses the soldier and the worker until you ask for another unit to be placed. On a column you add, Dropdown still picks one. Multi choice uses that same list, and a row can tick as many as it needs. A row or column you add is kept here too. It does not change the match until it is linked up.

Buildings

Building Graphic model (map view) Graphic model Unit production Time to build Cost Footprint size In use Look

Cost, build time, and footprint are kept in this browser. Reload the match after a change. They apply the next time that building is placed. The manor is already standing, so a footprint change shows after a reload. The blacksmith is the preview on the map and is not on the build bar yet. Time is in real seconds. Footprint is the width and depth of that building on the map, in metres. It starts as the size of the picture already in the match. Change it and reload, and the building changes with it. Tick In use yourself once you have seen that building placed and are happy with it. Graphic model (map view) lists House 1 to House 20 from the free pack, and keeps the picture already on that row. Pick a house, then reload the match. The map shows that house, sized to the footprint on this row. The other graphic stays the first-person picture. The ticks beside the graphics are kept here and do not change the match. Unit production can list more than one military or economical unit. + unit adds another. A building trains the units listed on its row. Wood is the cost the match charges. + resource adds another, such as gold, and the match charges that too. Purpose can be more than one of military, defence, store resources, and other. On a column you add, Dropdown still picks one. Multi choice uses that same list, and a row can tick as many as it needs. A row or column you add is kept here too. It does not change the match until it is linked up.

Creatures

These are the animals that already have a walk or a trot, plus the zombie. The blob is gone. Brute was the old faction name. The zombie is the woods figure, and See shows it. Horse is the one already in the match. Horse 2 walks and runs. Cart horse and the wooden cart are from last night, and Horse with cart is those two in one picture. Pick the horse on the Horse+Cart row. None of that is in the match yet. The horse in the match uses the Horse row. Defence on that row is taken off a hit, the same way as a unit. Change its base size and reload the match, and that horse changes with it. Base size is width by depth in metres. The other animals are not on the map, so their size is kept until one is placed. Tick In use yourself once you have seen that one placed and are happy with it. Food is the cost the match charges for the horse. + resource adds another, such as gold, and the match charges that too. Type is a dropdown. Add the choices in that column’s heading. Dropdown picks one of them. Multi choice uses the same list and a row can tick as many as it needs. A column named Created at ticks buildings, and a row can tick more than one. None of that changes the match yet. A row or column you add is kept here too. It does not change the match until it is linked up.

Player

Player In Game use Graphic model used Stats Animations Cost Base size In use

This is the person you walk as in first person. Health, sword damage, bow damage, and move speed are kept in this browser. Reload the match after a change. Health is the full bar, and what you return with after you fall. Sword damage is a swing. Bow damage is a shot. Defence is taken off a hit before it reaches you, and your shot is reduced by the target’s defence. Move is how fast you walk. Jump is how hard you leave the ground. It starts at the jump you have now. Change it, commit, and reload the match. Base size is the width and depth of the person you walk as, in metres. Change it and reload the match. In use is kept here and does not change the match yet. A column you add is kept here too, until you say what it is for.

Campaign

Each faction keeps its own resources, units, and buildings. Team A and Team B are the two sides on the map. Change a number here and the match follows it: the stock, the people, and the extra buildings. The manors stay where they are. A side’s name is used as soon as you rename it. A faction you add is kept here until it has a side on the map. Soldier and worker are the people the match can field, and a military or economical unit you added is placed with that body. Reload the match once after this update so the current game picks up the page.
