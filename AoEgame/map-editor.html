Click the ground to plant the chosen tree or building. Team buildings are the real match buildings: Manor, Small house, Barracks, Farm, Salt works, Storehouse, and extras from the developer page. Pick a team, then one of those. They get that side's colour box and flag, and Save makes them that side's buildings in Game or Play test. Extra teams from the developer page are stored, but the live match still uses Team A and Team B. The Buildings list is scenery only, even if the model is the same. None keeps new Buildings-list kits as scenery. A building or a town shows under the cursor first. Q and E turn that preview a quarter turn: forward, right, back, left. A town is scenery, the same as a placed building. Drag a box to highlight several. Copy, type a name, and Keep stores that group. Click the name, then Q and E turn it, then click the ground. Erase removes the highlight, and while it is on a click or a box removes what is under it. Pick a path and drag to paint it. Lift rubs that paint back to grass. The brush goes up to 72 m. Show marks turns the colour on. Hide marks, or T, turns it off while you keep editing. Paint where units and buildings may go. Unmarked ground is closed. Red is units. Blue is buildings, and units can walk there too. Lift rubs a mark away. Clear all empties the objects on this map. Save writes the objects, the paths, and the marks, and the next Game or Play test starts a new match from only what you placed. No extra units or manors are added. The marks stay invisible in the match.
