Recruiting takes five minutes

The houses choose when to build, train, and march, and only march on those they are at war with. They gather about 40 soldiers before marching unless the enemy is weak, and build within 200 m of their last buildings, up to 500 m out. Rural communities keep no soldiers and ask the nearest friendly house for protection. You belong to no house: whoever you strike treats you as an enemy until you die.
