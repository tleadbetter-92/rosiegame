Campaign

Recruiting takes two minutes

A village holds 30. A villager takes 1 and a soldier takes 2. A fisherman or a woodcutter costs 50 food and a soldier costs 70. Training takes two minutes, and they can be trained at the same time. Fishermen stay home and bring 10 food back from the sea. Woodcutters leave the store, chop the nearest tree for thirty seconds, and bring back 10 wood. A villager spends that wood to repair a damaged or broken gate. The houses choose when. Sometimes a house marches on another village, and it leaves a tenth of its soldiers behind. The last house standing takes the town.
